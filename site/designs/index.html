Designs

One set of core modules (the API), several ways to use it. Each design runs on a synthetic mailbox first, clearly marked, so anyone can try it without signing in. Live mode, with your own Google sign-in in your own browser, follows once this site's address is added to the Google sign-in client.

  • Send

    Pick a contact, see the draft, edit it, press Send. The outbox rules hold: one recipient, no Bcc, at most 20 a day, and you confirm before anything goes.

  • Signed batch

    Emails you signed off elsewhere (for example in the CRM) are checked against your pinned key and sent on one confirmation per batch, with PDF, PNG or JPEG attachments.

  • Onboarding drafts

    Three emails Dinis sends himself to a new site owner: the QR code, your uploads are in, your site is ready. Demo only, with a fictional owner.
