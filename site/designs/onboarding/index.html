Sovereign Website mail Onboarding drafts

Demo mode
Designs Send API Policy
Demo mode, a proposal. Fictional owner, reserved example domains, placeholders in square brackets. Nothing here is sent, and no agent sends mail: Dinis reads each draft, fills in the placeholders and presses Send himself.

One draft for each stage of the onboarding workflow (invited, qr-sent, uploads-in, designs-ready, live, editing; the stage list is the CRM's and may change), each linkable by its stage id. Dinis changes the wording to his own, fills in the placeholders and sends from his own mail client. The owner's real name, address and files stay in the CRM, never in this repository.

Invited

To: alex@example.org (fictional owner)
Subject: An invitation to build your site with us

Hello [first name],

It was good to talk. As we discussed, I would like to help you get your own site online, and the first step is simple: I will send you a QR code that opens a private space made for you. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

If you would like to go ahead, reply "yes" and I will send it.

Best wishes,
Dinis

QR sent

To: alex@example.org (fictional owner)
Subject: Welcome: your own space for your site

Hello Alex,

Thank you for your time on our call. As promised, here is the QR code that opens your own space for your site. It is private to you, and there is nothing to install and no account to make.

[QR code image, attached by Dinis]

When you open it you can:
- choose what kind of site you would like and see an example;
- add your photos and videos, and a few lines about yourself;
- come back whenever you want to add or change something.

Nothing you add is used on your site until you have said, in plain words, that you are happy for it to be public. You stay in charge.

If anything is unclear, just reply to this email.

Best wishes,
Dinis
